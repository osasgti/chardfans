## CUE SHEET RULES (apply when building `LieVideo`-style sequencing for this video)
- **Hits after words:** bursts at `line.end + 0.05 s`; frays may land on the word (they're sad, not a punch).
- **Max 3 SFX at once:** spool + hum + one accent.
- **Pre-lap ambience** 0.4 s before every set change; crossfade 1.2 s.
- **Panning:** thread SFX pan by the thread midpoint's screen x (−0.6…+0.6).
- **Silence:** after P039, mute everything except a 0.05 room tone for 0.8 s.
- **Loudness:** VO −16 LUFS per line, master −14 LUFS, TP ≤ −1.5.
